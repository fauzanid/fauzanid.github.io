Privacy Policy

Paperloom: PDF Scanner & Editor · Last updated November 25, 2026

Short version: Paperloom does not collect any data. Your documents, scans, photos and signatures are processed only on your iPhone and are never uploaded, shared or sold.

Documents and files

When you open a PDF from Files or another app, Paperloom copies it into its private storage on your iPhone so it can work on it. Every tool (merge, split, organize, compress and sign) runs on your device using Apple's PDFKit. The files you create are saved in the app's private storage until you delete them or the app. You choose if and where to share them with the iOS share sheet.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you start a scan, through Apple's document scanner. Scanned pages are turned into a PDF on your iPhone. Paperloom only sees the photos you pick with the system photo picker, and uses them only to build the PDF you asked for.

Signatures

Your drawn signature is added to the PDF you are signing. Paperloom doesn't keep a copy of it anywhere else.

Data we collect

None. Paperloom has no account system, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send your data to any server. The developer has no way to see your documents or how you use the app.

Subscriptions and payments

Paperloom Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and manages your subscription. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only asks the App Store on your device whether you have an active subscription. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Paperloom is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
