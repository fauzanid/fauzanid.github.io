Gem Grid Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Short version: Your game progress stays on your device. We (the developer) run no servers and collect nothing ourselves. Gem Grid shows optional reward videos provided by Google AdMob; Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure those ads, and, only if you allow tracking, to personalize them. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. If you buy Remove Ads, the app stops loading ads.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following only on your device: best scores, coins, power-ups and unlocked themes; adventure stars, daily challenge history and streak; statistics, achievements and the sound setting; and which purchases have been delivered (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, store transaction IDs) so nothing is granted twice. This data is not sent to us. It may be included in your own device backups under Apple's terms. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Gem Grid uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to offer optional rewarded videos (for example "Watch ad to revive", "Double coins" and a free daily power-up). Videos only play when you tap one of these buttons. To serve, measure and prevent fraud on these ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt, otherwise the per-vendor identifier (IDFV) and other non-tracking signals;
  • Usage and diagnostics data: ad interactions (views, taps, completions), app launch and performance information, crash and diagnostic data from the ads SDK;
  • Coarse location derived from your IP address, and general device information (model, OS version, language).

Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: the first time ads are needed, iOS asks whether Gem Grid may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalized based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws): where required, a Google consent form asks how your data may be used for ads. You can change your choice later from Shop → Ad privacy choices in the app.
  • Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads stops the app from loading ads; the ad rewards become free.
  • Ads are limited to a "PG" content rating.

In-app purchases

Coins, the Starter Pack and Remove Ads are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, Apple ID, card or billing details. The app only receives a transaction from StoreKit confirming which product was bought, and stores the transaction ID on your device to deliver it once. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

Gem Grid is rated for all ages but is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Ads are restricted to a "PG" content rating.

Permissions

The only permission the app may ask for is the iOS tracking permission described above. It does not access your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location. It uses the haptic engine for vibration feedback during play.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not receive data from Google about you. The data described under "Advertising" is collected directly by Google under its own policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will be reflected in an app update.
