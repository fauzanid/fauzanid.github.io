Gem Grid Support

Need help with Gem Grid: Block Puzzle? We're happy to help.

Contact: email fauzan428email@gmail.com. Please include your device model, iOS version and a short description of the problem. We usually reply within 2 business days.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the main menu, tap SHOP, scroll to the bottom and tap RESTORE PURCHASES. Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Remove Ads and the Starter Pack are restored this way. Coin packs are consumable, so they are delivered once and cannot be restored.

I bought something but didn't receive it

Keep the app open for a few seconds with an internet connection, then tap RESTORE PURCHASES in the shop. If it still hasn't arrived, email us with the date of purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my progress saved? Will I lose it?

Your scores, coins, levels, streak and themes are saved on your device and kept when you update the app. They are not stored online, so deleting the app deletes your progress. Progress is included in your iPhone or iPad backup (iCloud or computer), so restoring a backup to a new device brings it back.

About ads

Gem Grid never shows forced ads. Short videos only play when you choose a reward: Watch ad to revive, Double coins after a level, or the daily free power-up. Buying Remove Ads makes all of those rewards free with no videos. Ads are provided by Google AdMob and limited to a PG content rating.

How do I change ad tracking or consent?

Tracking: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Gem Grid. Consent (where required): in the app, Shop → Ad privacy choices. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does the game work offline?

Yes. All game modes work without a connection. The internet is only needed for optional reward videos and App Store purchases.

The sound isn't playing

Check the sound button on the title screen and turn off your device's silent switch / Silent Mode, which mutes game audio.
