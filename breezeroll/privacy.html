Privacy Policy

Breezeroll: Photo Cleaner · Last updated November 25, 2026

Short version: Your photos and videos are processed only on your iPhone and are never uploaded, shared or sold. We (the developer) run no servers and collect nothing ourselves. Free users can choose to watch optional reward videos provided by Google AdMob; Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to show and measure those ads, and, only if you allow tracking, to personalize them. Breezeroll Pro subscribers never see ads.

Photos and videos

Breezeroll asks for access to your photo library so it can find duplicates, similar photos, screenshots and large videos. To do this it creates small visual fingerprints of your photos on your device. Fingerprints, file sizes and your choices (for example which items you kept) are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone, and are deleted when you delete the app.

Breezeroll never deletes anything without your confirmation. Deletions go through Apple's system prompt, and deleted items stay in the Photos app's "Recently Deleted" album for 30 days.

Data we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. Breezeroll has no account system, no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own, and it does not send your photos and videos to any server. The developer has no way to see them or how you use the app. The only third party that may receive data is Google, for the optional ads described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When a free user reaches the daily limit, Breezeroll offers an optional rewarded video: watch it to get 20 more deletions that day, or one more video compression (up to 3 times a day). Videos only play when you tap "Watch an ad". The ads SDK does not start before that, and it never starts for Breezeroll Pro subscribers. To serve, measure and prevent fraud on these ads, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt, otherwise the per-vendor identifier (IDFV) and other non-tracking signals;
  • Usage and diagnostics data: ad interactions (views, taps, completions), app launch and performance information, crash and diagnostic data from the ads SDK;
  • Coarse location derived from your IP address, and general device information (model, OS version, language).

Google never receives your photos and videos. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ads are limited to a "PG" content rating.

Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency: before the first ad, iOS asks whether Breezeroll may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and ads are not personalized based on your activity in other companies' apps and websites. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws): where required, a Google consent form asks how your data may be used for ads. You can change your choice later in Breezeroll → Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • No ads at all: don't tap "Watch an ad", or subscribe to Breezeroll Pro; the ads SDK then never starts.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not receive data from Google about you. The data described under "Advertising" is collected directly by Google under its own policies.

Subscriptions and payments

Breezeroll Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment and manages your subscription. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only asks the App Store on your device whether you have an active subscription. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Breezeroll is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and ads are restricted to a "PG" content rating.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
